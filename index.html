The countdown starts when you finish

Water the plants today, get reminded again in five days. Not on a fixed date — five days after you actually did it. Fall behind and nothing piles up; the clock simply waits for you.

Three times a week, no streaks to break. Read twenty minutes, go for a walk, practice guitar — the app nudges you toward the goal and shrugs when life gets in the way.
