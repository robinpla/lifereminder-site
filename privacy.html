Privacy Policy

Last updated: EFFECTIVE_DATE

Life Reminder is made by Gryning Studio. This policy explains what the app does with your information — which, in short, is nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Life Reminder does not collect, transmit or store any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising. The app does not request internet access.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — tasks, reminders, habits, settings — is stored only on your device. We cannot see it, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you uninstall the app, that data is deleted along with it.

Exports and backups

If you choose to export your tasks, the app creates a file on your device. That file is yours: where you keep it, share it or back it up is entirely under your control.

Notifications

The app uses your phone's notification system to deliver reminders you set. These are generated on your device and are not routed through any server.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and since it collects no data, no personal information from anyone is gathered.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
